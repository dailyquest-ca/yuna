so the test must not assume it. A second falsifier is added:

> **Rank-lag falsifier.** Run the center reading the rank and the base state on session *i − k*
> while executing on session *i*, for k ∈ {1, 2, 5}. A door responding to a slow state should
> barely notice being read a day or a week late. A result that moves materially was riding a
> specific-day effect, which is precisely the failure a start-offset test cannot see.

Both interpretation calls the specification required are pinned in `src/concentrated.py` and
tested:

* **Clustering method (§2 requires one pre-registered):** single-linkage at ρ > 0.70 over 126
  sessions. Chosen because it merges readily and therefore caps more aggressively than
  complete-linkage — a rider meant to prevent a 1.84-effective-bet book should err toward calling
  names related rather than unrelated.
* **Effective-bets floor:** binds from the fifth name onward. A three-name book cannot have five
  effective bets under any correlation structure, so a literal reading refuses every entry and
  the book never fills. Below five names only the cluster cap applies. Where correlation cannot
  be measured the rider abstains and logs, rather than blocking — the alternative is a
  liquidity-and-history filter masquerading as a diversification rule.

## 0 · The design thesis (why this arm exists)

The frequency axis proved two things at once: raw return rises all the way to daily (the 12-1÷vol signal is real and decays fast) and Sharpe peaks at bi-monthly (calendar turnover eats the freshness faster than it earns past that point). The daily cell's flaw is structural: it **welds the observation clock to the transaction clock** — re-ranking daily *and* forcing the book to equal the top-8 daily, which re-trades rank flicker at the margin of a slow signal ($416k of spreads, −56% expected DD, Sharpe below the ETF).

A6 separates the clocks. **Observe every session; transact only on gates.** Entries via a state-door (the strongest *qualifying* name when a slot frees — not "whatever broke out today," the design flaw that produced §10's 1.54%). Exits via the trail (primary) or a rank hysteresis band (a name is held until it *travels*, not until it flickers). No calendar exists in the rule, so rebalance-timing luck dies by construction rather than by tranching.

Pre-registered predictions: turnover **< 100 names/yr** at center; mean CAGR **≥ the bi-monthly candidate's 33.37%**; start-offset spread **≤ 3 points** by construction; bootstrap-median DD **≤ −40%** required, ≤ −35% aspiration.

## 1 · A6 center specification

**Universe & rank:** top-500 US names by point-in-time dollar volume, price ≥ $5; rank = 12-1 momentum ÷ realized volatility.

**Book:** N = **12**. Entry size = equity/12, funded from park. Weights drift; **no conformance trading, no trims**. Idle capital parks in **SPMO**; if no qualifying candidate exists when a slot frees, the slot stays parked — never force an entry.

**Observation clock:** every session. **Transaction clock:** gates only, all executions at next morning's open.

**Entry gate (the state-door).** A slot is free → buy the **highest-ranked** name satisfying: (1) rank ≤ **15**; (2) valid-base state — close within **10%** of its 252-session high close, close > 50-day SMA, and 50-day SMA today > 50-day SMA ten sessions ago; (3) the correlation rider. If the top qualifier fails the rider, step down the rank to the next qualifier.

**Exit gates (any one fires; next-open execution):** (1) **Trail** — §3.2 as written. (2) **Rank band** — rank > **40** at the nightly observation. Between 15 and 40, flicker generates zero trades.

**Costs:** the sourced curve, charged on every leg including park round-trips.

## 2 · Mandatory rider — effective-bets floor at formation

At every entry the resulting book must satisfy **effective bets ≥ 5** (§2.2 formula, trailing 126 sessions) and **≤ 2 names per correlation cluster** (pairwise ρ > 0.7). Gates **formation only — never forces exits**; continuous effective bets is *reported*, not enforced.

## 4 · Falsifiers and pass bars

- **Start-offset test:** center at 5 offsets {0, +10, +21, +42, +63 sessions}. **Kill: spread > 6 points.**
- **Rank-lag test (amendment):** k ∈ {1, 2, 5}. Same kill bar.
- **Livability:** report names/yr and minutes/week. **Flag any cell > 2 trades/week regardless of CAGR.**
- **§2.5 in full, SPMO the benchmark:** bootstrap-median CAGR > SPMO ex-top-3, DSR ≥ 0.95 at the live ledger count, OOS cut, bootstrap-median DD ≤ −40%.
- **Kill:** bootstrap-median < SPMO ⇒ hold-the-ETF stands; turnover > 150/yr ⇒ redesign, don't tune.
