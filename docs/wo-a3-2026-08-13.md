## 2 · The push study — measure every gate before adopting any

The capture audit's episode walk, extended and written to a table (`push_study`, migration 046)
so gates are measured on our own census rather than argued from papers. One row per episode —
**completed push, failed breakout, or unresolved** — with:

- **Features, all computed on bars at or before the breakout day** (look-ahead-safe by
  construction): 90-day exponential regression slope (annualized) and R² and their product
  (Clenow); share of up-days over 126 sessions and daily-return volatility over 90 (frog-in-
  the-pan smoothness); largest single-day move over 90 (MAX/lottery); ATR(20) as a fraction of
  price (the `trend_vol` key); 126- and 252-session prior gains (the practitioners'
  demonstrated-move precondition); 50-session median dollar volume; raw price; distance from
  the 50-day MA; sessions since the name's prior completed push; and the market regime (S&P
  above/below its 200-session SMA) on the breakout day.
- **Outcome labels:** completed or failed; gain reached; sessions to resolution; and — the
  ride question answered without running a single engine backtest — whether the episode
  **survived** each candidate exit to completion: a 3×ATR(20) stop from the breakout level
  (run 58's clipper), a 10-day-MA close-below trail, a 20-day-MA trail, and the name's own
  100-day MA (Clenow's exit).

Candidate gates logged for the §2.5(d) burden: **12 features × the exit set** as enumerated
above. Analysis is SQL over the table: each gate's lift on P(complete) against the base rate,
and each exit's survival rate among completed pushes. Gates that show no lift die here, at the
cost of zero trials.

## 3 · A3 — "hold the leaders" (the arm family the evidence converges on)

Machinery pinned only where the evidence is already unambiguous; the selection thresholds are
**pinned from the push study's measured lifts before the first run**, and recorded in an
amendment to this WO at that moment:

- **Selection:** rank by measured trend quality — 90-day exponential slope × R² and/or the
  smoothness measure, per whichever the study shows carries lift on our census. Universe floors
  as E3 (ADDV ≥ $10M, raw ≥ $5).
- **Entry:** a name enters when it is in the top-N of the quality ranking and passes the M2
  template; re-entry on any fresh qualification. No score floor beyond the ranking itself.
- **Exits:** trend death, not entry noise — the name leaves when it decays out of the ranking
  band and/or closes below its own long MA (per the study's survival table). A **catastrophe
  stop** stands far outside noise (the Protect layer never sleeps — plan §4.0), declared, wide
  enough that the study says winners survive it.
- **Risk:** ATR-parity sizing (equal risk per name); **vol-governed gross exposure** — scale
  momentum exposure toward a target strategy volatility per Barroso–Santa-Clara. **This
  revives WO-17 by ruling**: run 58 measured the unmanaged disease (−62% bootstrap tail,
  kurtosis ~14), and the published fix (Sharpe 0.53→0.97, kurtosis 18.2→2.7) addresses the
  exact clause A2 died on. The governor's parameters are declared in the preset, not tuned.
- **Chassis:** §2.1 park on every arm, unchanged.
- **Bars:** §2.5 with `dd_bar = −0.34` (E3's bar carries over — the family must be shallower
  than the index's −34%), capture audit on every run.
- **Kill:** bootstrap-median CAGR below the best E4 blend, or the DD bar broken ⇒ the cell is
  dead. Ladder: one axis at a time, announced in the amendment with the center.
