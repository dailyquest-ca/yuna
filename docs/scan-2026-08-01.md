decimals. `db.nav_cad` already values every holding with `join lateral (… order by d desc limit 1)`:

The audit's `$300.41` was never a bar. AVGO's own history has no close near it since March. It is
`16,425.73 ÷ 1.402 ÷ 39` — the correct market value divided by an **incorrect share count of 39**.

**What does not change:** item 2's canary is §4.2 law and gets built regardless. It would not have
caught this — the price was never wrong — so a second canary is added: **book quantity reconciled
against the broker record at every Sunday R4**. Share count is the axis that actually failed, and it
is invisible to a price check.

## Part D — eight defects on neither list

**N1 · `score.py:145` — the C1 failure reason is inverted, so failing names lose it.**
`c1_fail_reason=(c1why if c1 else None)` keeps the reason when the gate *passes* and discards it when
it *fails*. Live proof: AVGO's fundamentals row reads `net issuance 4.7%/yr`; its bench row reads
`c1_pass=false, c1_fail_reason=null`. Same for CNQ.TO, whose real reason is
`net issuance 22.2%/yr; net debt growing faster than EBITDA`. This defeats §3.1's requirement that
the gap be named on the C2 memo, and it is the single cheapest fix in the scan.

**N2 · The monthly L0 rebuild wipes `universe.industry` and `market_cap_usd`.**
2,108 of 2,762 L0 names have a null industry — and 2,077 of them have one sitting in `fundamentals`.
Only names re-swept that month get theirs back (the 08-01 sweep wrote 131 rows). Consequences, all
live: MCN's **industry group strength scores a flat neutral 50 for ~76% of the field** — one of three
equal components is a constant — and §2.2's 2-per-group cap counts every one of them as the same
`unknown` group. This is the largest single distortion the scan found.

**N3 · `armed` carries a score snapshot that can contradict the queue.**
Live: RS.US is `score 79.88` in `armed` and `69.67` in both `candidates` and `queue`; BMA, FRO, GRDN,
PRM and YPF sit in `armed` but not in the queue at all. `duties` truncates and rewrites `armed`
nightly; `weekly-rank` rewrites the queue Saturday afternoon — so from Saturday 12:00 UTC until the
next nightly, `armed` is stale by construction. A session composing R1 off `armed` prints last
night's MCN. Tickets must re-read the score at write time. This is almost certainly the true origin
of item 6's "two MCNs in one run".

**N4 · `db.nav_cad` overwrites `per_ticker` instead of accumulating.**
`per_ticker[tk] = cad` — one ticker held in two accounts reports only the second. Latent today
(one-position-one-account makes it unreachable by design) but the NAV detail JSON would understate
silently, so it gets fixed alongside item 15 rather than left as a trap.

**N5 · Phase 0 step 2a has never been applied to the live book.**
Every `book` row except CNQ.TO reads `sleeve='unassigned'`, and `theme` is null on all seven. §6 2a
assigns a sleeve to every holding; §2.2 records theme on the book. Until this runs, sleeve ceilings,
theme caps and the 2-per-group check are all computing against `unassigned`.

**N6 · The debt tripwire is the dominant C1 killer** — 864 names, more than the 623 that pass. V4b is
not a marginal correction; it reopens a third of the universe.

**N7 · `reinvest_rate` is exactly 0 on 895 names**, including ANET, AVGO, VRT and CNQ — `learnings.md`
#16, confirmed at scale. The waterfall resolves it without a special clause, as the plan says.

**N8 · 24 names carry a null `statement_currency`** and are currently scored as though the currency
were known.

## Part E — the one interpretation I need ruled

§3.1 now says effective shares = **"vendor cap ÷ the close on the cap's report date, frozen with the
filing."** The vendor's `MarketCapitalization` is a *live* figure with no report date of its own — it
is whatever it was when we pulled the document. My reading, and what I will build unless corrected:
**at each fundamentals pull we snapshot the cap, the close of that pull date, and the effective-share
count they imply, and store all three on the filing row.** The hurdle then moves only when a filing
moves it. Corporate actions re-derive via the per-ticker call exception, exactly as written.

Second, smaller: §2.6 gives higher-yield US compounders a preference for the RRSP, and Zak confirmed
it — but "higher-yield" has no number. `fundamentals` does not currently extract a dividend yield;
EODHD serves one in `Highlights`. I will add the field and gate the preference on a config threshold,
default **2.0%**, pending a ruling.
