## 4 · LESSONS — the scar tissue (read before touching anything)

**EODHD**
1. **Pull broad, filter locally.** Symbol/section filters on fundamentals are unreliable; client-side filtering is the law of this codebase.
2. Screener `offset` hard-caps at **999** → paginate by **market-cap band descent**, never raw offset.
3. **One condition per field per screener call** (dual cap bounds risk 422); move the floor client-side.
4. Screener `market_capitalization` is **null for most US names** — the screener is a *decorator*, never a census. Full-tape truth = `eod-bulk-last-day` (≈1 call per 1,000 symbols).
5. Never let a null cap update a descending ceiling (one bad row collapses the sweep to `< $1`).
6. Earnings calendar is entitled on All-In-One (verified: 3,638 rows Aug 4–5 incl. AMD 8/4, SHOP 8/5, MRK 8/4 BMO).

**GitHub Actions**
7. Cron `dom` and `dow` are **OR'd** — `0 10 1-7 * 6` fires every day 1–7 *and* every Saturday. Schedule weekly + guard the date **in-job**.
8. A dispatch seconds after a push can check out the **stale** ref. Sleep ~20 s, then assert the run's `head_sha` equals the SHA you pushed. Every time.
9. Run/job **log downloads are unreachable** (302 → Azure blob → 403 even unauthenticated). Do not burn time there.
10. Therefore: **the heartbeat is the only reliable diagnostic.** Fatal handlers embed tracebacks; the autopsy-on-failure step catches pre-heartbeat deaths (one transient DB-connect blip already proved its worth). Debug from `runs.detail`, always.

**Platform**
11. Supabase MCP is **read-only** — correct posture, not a bug. Machine writes via repo; sessions inspect. (Revisit only for Phase C brief-writing, per plan.)
12. Empty-repo first commit needs the **Contents API** (git-data returns 409 until the ref exists).
13. Storage: 2.1M price rows ≈ **285 MB** of 500 MB. The monthly prune of non-L0 bars is not optional. Backups exclude bars by design.
14. Claude sandbox: ~170 s per bash call; egress allowlist blocks eodhd.com and Supabase directly — **nothing can be smoke-tested locally; everything runs through Actions.** (Claude Code on Zak's machine may differ — retest your own egress before assuming.)
15. Helper scripts (`push.py`, `gha.py`) lived in the old sandbox and **do not transfer**. In Claude Code, prefer real `git` + `gh`.

**Process**
16. Broker screenshot beats stored records — reconcile against reality, then migrate the correction (`003` is the precedent).
17. Verify magnitudes, not just green: a green census with 688 names was **wrong three times** before it was right at 2,783. Green + plausible + cross-checked, or it didn't happen.
18. Six census takes, every one diagnosed from her own heartbeat in under a minute. The design philosophy — *the database is the better morgue* — is now empirically earned. Keep it.
