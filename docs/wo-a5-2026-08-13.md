## 1 · The drawdown bar — RULING REQUIRED (Zak, §4.5)

**A4 declared no drawdown bar.** E3's centre was killed by exactly that clause at -34%; this arm
never faced it, so `proven` above means proven on return, robustness and the OOS cut, and silent
on risk. A bar must exist before the arm goes further, and it must be set now rather than after
the ladder below runs.

**Proposed, and derivable rather than chosen:** the sleeve's entire justification (§2.4.1) is
beating the park. A sleeve that risks more than the park has to pay for the difference. So the
bar is **the park's own drawdown distribution, measured the same way**:

- **Clause 1** — bootstrap median drawdown must not exceed SPMO's **-30.95%**
- **Clause 2** — bootstrap 5th-percentile drawdown must not exceed SPMO's **-42.86%**

Measured today, `lg8_semi_trail` **fails both, marginally**: -31.0% against -30.95% (5 basis
points) and -44.4% against -42.86% (1.5 points). Stated plainly because it is the uncomfortable
reading and the one that matters: **the champion buys 10.6 points of CAGR for slightly more tail
risk than the park, and whether that trade is acceptable is a risk-posture call — Zak's alone.**

Three ways it could be ruled, all legitimate, none of them mine to pick:

1. **As proposed** — the sleeve must not risk more than the park. The champion fails and the arm
   needs a tighter trail before it goes further.
2. **With a stated tolerance** — e.g. the sleeve may exceed the park's tail by N points because
   it returns 10.6 more. If so, N is a number Zak sets, in the plan, before the ladder runs.
3. **On a different statistic entirely** — e.g. time-to-recovery, or drawdown in CAD after FX.

Until this is ruled, **nothing below changes what the arm is allowed to be called.** The ladder
runs regardless — it is about whether the result is real, not whether the risk is acceptable —
but no cell may be described as passing a bar that does not exist.

## 2 · The realism gaps, and which are closable today

### 2.1 The stop cannot be filled at the stop (CLOSABLE — the data is there)

`concentrated.simulate` tests §3.2's trail on the **close** and fills at the **next close**,
because the tape was loaded without a range. `prices` carries `high` and `low` on all 8,858,709
rows, and `capture_audit.load_tape(with_range=True)` already returns them.

This is not a small approximation and it does **not** obviously flatter:

- filling at the stop rather than a session later is **better** on a name that keeps falling
- but a real stop also fires on an **intraday spike the close recovered from** — a shakeout the
  close-based test never sees — which is **worse**, and is the classic reason mechanical stops
  underperform their backtest
- and a **gap through the stop** fills at the open, not the stop

All three are now testable. Pre-registered: the ladder below runs on close-based fills (as
measured) **and** on intraday fills, and both are reported. If the champion's CAGR falls by more
than 5 points on intraday fills, the close-based number was an artefact of the fill model and the
intraday number is the one that counts.

### 2.2 Earnings are invisible to this arm (CLOSABLE, lower priority)

The concentrated book holds through every earnings date with an 8% initial stop. §3.2 legislates
a hold-through rule requiring a +8% cushion for a reason: an earnings gap does not respect a
stop. The store has an earnings calendar. Not in this round; named so it is not forgotten.

### 2.3 The window has one bear market and cannot be extended (NOT CLOSABLE — needs a backfill)

`prices` runs from **2003-08-18**, but only for SPY and two other ETFs. The **stock universe
begins 2016-08**: June 2016 has 3 tickers, June 2017 has 3,620. So the tape covers 2018, 2020,
2022 — and no 2008, and critically **no 2009**, which is the momentum crash Barroso &
Santa-Clara built their governor for and the single most informative regime this strategy could
be shown.

Closing it means a bulk EODHD pull of ~3,000 names x 13 years. That is a real API cost and a real
ingest job, and it is **the highest-value item on this list by a distance** — a momentum result
that has never seen a momentum crash is a result with a known blind spot, however good its
bootstrap looks. Scoped here, not run: it needs Zak's go-ahead on cost before anything fetches.

## 3 · The robustness ladder — DECLARED BEFORE RUNNING

A champion picked from twenty-two cells must be a **plateau**, not a spike. Every axis moves one
step either side of `lg8_semi_trail`; the arm is robust if the surface around it is smooth and
fitted if it is not.

| axis | champion | one step down | one step up |
|---|---|---|---|
| names held | 8 | 6 | 10 |
| pool (top-K by ADDV) | 500 | 250 | 750 |
| trail band | 10% | 8% | 12% |
| arm threshold | +15% | +12% | +18% |
| initial stop | 8% | 6% | 10% |
| euphoria band | 5% | 4% | 6% |
| clock | semi-annual | quarterly | annual |

**Pre-registered reading, so it cannot be rationalised afterwards:**

- **robust** — every neighbour within **5 CAGR points** of the champion, and no neighbour's
  bootstrap median drawdown more than **5 points** worse
- **fitted** — any single-step neighbour losing more than 10 CAGR points
- **anything between** — reported as-is, with the axis named; a strategy sensitive on ONE axis is
  a strategy with one parameter that needs a source in the plan, not a dead one

Note what this ladder is not: it is not a search for a better cell. If a neighbour beats the
champion, that is **evidence about the surface**, not a new champion — promoting it would be
re-running the same twenty-two-cell selection with extra steps, and the deflated Sharpe already
prices that.

### 3.1 Cost sensitivity

§2.2's spread curve at **1x, 2x and 4x**. Momentum at 16.7 entries a year is not cost-fragile in
principle; "in principle" is not a measurement. Pre-registered: if 2x costs remove more than 3
CAGR points, the arm is thinner than its trade count suggests.

## 4 · What is deliberately NOT in this work order

- **No new cell families.** The grid is closed. Adding an axis after seeing the answer is the
  search the deflation exists to punish, and 63 trials is already a wide net.
- **No parameter tuning.** Every constant in the champion traces to §3.2's own text. If the
  ladder shows a neighbour is better, the finding is "the surface tilts that way", and any change
  to the law is a plan edit first.
- **No production role.** §2.4.2's concentration review has not run on this arm, the drawdown bar
  above is unruled, and nothing in this repo places an order regardless.
