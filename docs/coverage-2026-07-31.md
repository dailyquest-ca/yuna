## What this pass found

**48 rules enforced and pinned. 17 enforced without a test. 12 not implemented**, every one of
them already named in the roadmap's Phase J or L, plus two deferrals (analyst-target sanity flag,
which is low value; and the RRSP placement rule, which lives in `phase0.py` pending its rebuild).

Two observations worth recording rather than fixing:

1. **§2.3's 25% single-name cap cannot bind in practice.** Momentum sizing is clipped by the 12%
   band ceiling and compounders top out at 15%, so nothing the machine sizes can reach 25%. It is
   enforced and tested — the test has to override the config to 5% to make it fire — but it is a
   backstop against a future sizing change, not a live constraint.
2. **The theme cap and the 2-per-brief throttle are session rules by design**, so they cannot be
   unit-tested here. They are specified in `sessions/R1_preopen.md` and will only ever be as good
   as that runbook. That is the plan's own architecture (§2.2: theme is judgment), not a gap — but
   it does mean two §2 caps live in prose rather than in code, and that is worth knowing.
